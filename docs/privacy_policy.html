Warranty Wallet Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Data Storage

Warranty Wallet stores all device, warranty, invoice, and photo data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

In-App Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. Coin balances and purchased items are stored locally on your device.

Remote Configuration

The app may fetch a remote configuration file from https://api2.blwsmartware.net/R225.json to enable or disable Google Play billing. No device data is sent in this request.
